About

I'm a software engineer in Jaipur, about three years in. I started with Flutter and Node.js, and these days most of my energy goes into AI: building services where a language model does the reading and writing, and plain code checks everything it can.

The project I'm proudest of is a RAG microservice that reads whole school textbooks and turns them into exam papers and quizzes teachers can trust. Alongside it I built the school app it plugs into, now running across 30+ branches, a cognitive training platform where a few milliseconds really matter, and a mobile app for a mining business.

I studied Computer Science at JECRC and published a research paper on spatial computing in VR. I like the part of the job where an app leaves my laptop and real people start depending on it.

How I Work

  1. Plan before code. For anything big I write the design down first, with the decisions and the reasons, so the build has something to be checked against.
  2. AI tools as teammates. I use Claude, Codex and Antigravity every day to research, write code, review it and test it in parallel. I stay the one who decides and verifies.
  3. Trust, but check in code. If a computer can verify it, a model doesn't get the final word: totals, answers, schemas and permissions are all checked by code.
  4. Write down every fix. Each bug I fix gets a short note on why it happened, how I fixed it and how to avoid it next time. Those notes are where most of my lessons come from.
